Support

Questions, ideas or a problem? We read every message.

Write to info@racket-sports.app. It helps if you name the version of the app (Settings → About) and the sport.

The watch and the iPhone show different matches

The two sync directly with each other. Open the app on both devices while they are near each other and the newest state arrives within moments.

A purchase is missing

Purchases belong to your Apple Account. Tap Restore Purchases in the settings of the app or in the store. What you buy on the iPhone is also unlocked on the Apple Watch, and the other way round.

Cancel Premium or ask for a refund

Manage or cancel the subscription in the settings of your Apple Account, or through Manage Subscription in the settings of the app. Apple handles all payments: ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Heart rate or calories are missing

Turn on “Save as workout” in the settings of the app and allow access to Health when the watch asks at the start of the next match. You can change the access later in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

The place of a match is missing

The app keeps where a match is played only if it may read the location while it is in use. Change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Racket Sports.

Delete your data

Everything is stored on your devices. Delete a match by swiping it in the history, or delete the app to remove everything. Workouts saved to Health are deleted in the Health or Fitness app.

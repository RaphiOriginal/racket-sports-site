Racket Sports

Keep score on your Apple Watch, by the official rules.

Racket Sports keeps the score of your match on your Apple Watch, so you can keep your head in the game. One tap per point: the app knows the rules, who serves next, when to change ends and when the match is won.

Padel, tennis, table tennis, pickleball, badminton and squash. Singles and doubles.

Your iPhone keeps every finished match in one history, follows the match being counted on the watch, and shows your statistics.

No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Your matches stay on your devices.
